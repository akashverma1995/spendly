Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. When you use Spendly, we store the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions you provide.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or any data beyond what you explicitly enter into the app.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the Spendly service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries, and enabling you to manage your records. We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or any purpose beyond operating the app.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers. We take reasonable technical measures to protect it from unauthorized access. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

We retain your data only as long as your account is active. Deleted accounts and their data are permanently removed within 30 days.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data or expense records with any third parties. We do not integrate with advertising networks, analytics providers, or data brokers.

If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and notify you in advance.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us. We are committed to being transparent and will respond to all privacy-related inquiries promptly.
